To commemorate the release of The Warriors, Rockstar Games tapped New York City's own Retail Mafia to create exclusive, limited edition tee shirts and hoodies utilizing the classic, colorful gang logos from the film. Based on the 1979 Paramount Pictures film, Rockstar Games' The Warriors expands the stylized cinematic journey of the film into a gritty interactive experience set in 1970s New York. Each Retail Mafia designed Warriors tee shirt and hoody is produced on a strictly limited run of 500 pieces per design.

Each garment is adorned with a serialized hang tag highlighting the gang that inspired that design as well as an explanation of this unique collaboration.

The collection is available for purchase online, for a limited time only, from the Rockstar Warehouse and at select boutiques worldwide. For a current list of retail outlets please scroll down.
