MIDNIGHT CLUB LOS ANGELES AT THE DUB SHOW TOUR 2008
08.07.08
We are proud to be featuring Midnight Club Los Angeles all summer long at the Dub Show Tour 2008 - the world's largest custom auto show and concert tour, presented by renowned automotive customization enthusiasts, DUB Magazine. Midnight Club Los Angeles is the only videogame to feature cars customized by DUB. The DUB Show Tour is hitting stops across America this summer with the following upcoming dates:
  • Aug 23rd: Houston, TX (Reliant Center)
  • Aug 31st: San Jose, CA (San Jose Convention Center)
  • Sept 28th: San Diego, CA (San Diego Convention Center)
Visit the Rockstar Games: Midnight Club Los Angeles booth area at any of these shows to play the game in advance of its October 21st release date - play an exclusive demo featuring the Midnight Club Los Angeles Saleen S302E and get the chance to win limited-edition gear. For tickets and further info visit www.dubshowtour.com.
