Rockstar continues the tradition of ground-breaking Midnight Club soundtracks – hand-picking a
whopping collection of amped-up music perfect to race and ride to – from both cutting-edge
underground artists and established legends.

This time around for Midnight Club Los Angeles, extra attention has been paid to the
contemporary sonic landscape of California – artists from up and down the West Coast,
and songs that have been getting lots of radio and club burn in L.A. specifically. The end result is a massive,
yet carefully cultivated soundtrack that should appeal universally to rock, hip-hop, and electronic music
lovers worldwide and deliver a fitting, exhilirating score to complement all the intense in-game
street racing gameplay action.
